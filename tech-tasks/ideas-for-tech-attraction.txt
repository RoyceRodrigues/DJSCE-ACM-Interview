Ideas:

1. AR Treasure Hunt: Digital Reality Challenge

Concept:
A campus-wide treasure hunt where clues appear through augmented reality.

Participants:
Scan locations
Solve programming puzzles
Unlock hidden digital objects

Example:
A QR code near the library opens an AR model containing the next clue.

Resources Required:
AR frameworks (AR.js, Unity AR Foundation)
QR codes
Smartphones
Campus map

Technologies Showcased:
Augmented Reality, Mobile Development

Challenges:

3. Digital Forensics Investigation Lab:

Concept:
Participants become cyber investigators solving a fictional crime.

Scenario:
"A company's confidential data was leaked. Find the attacker."

Tasks:
Analyze logs
Recover deleted files
Inspect metadata
Identify attack methods
Resources Required
Sample disk images
Log files
Forensics tools
Computers

Tools:
Autopsy
Wireshark
FTK Imager

Technologies Showcased:
Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics
